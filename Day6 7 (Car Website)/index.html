Bugatti Veyron


Bugatti Veyron


The Bugatti Veyron is a legendary mid-engine hypercar built by Bugatti in France. It features an 8.0-liter quad-turbocharged W16 engine making 1,001 horsepower. It reaches a top speed of 407 km/h (253 mph) and costs over $1 million, pioneering the modern hypercar
